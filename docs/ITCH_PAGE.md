# AFTERPARTY

**A tiny robot. A very big mess.**

The party is over. Your shift is not.

You are MOP-01, a tiny cleaning robot taking care of a living room after a leaving party. Your human is asleep on the sofa. Before sunrise, vacuum the leftovers, recycle the mess into upgrades, and find four little memories that deserve to be kept.

Some things belong in a bin. Some things belong on a shelf.

## How to play

- **WASD / arrow keys:** move.
- **Hold Space:** vacuum.
- **Hold Shift:** quiet mode. Especially useful near the sofa.
- **E:** interact with glowing keepsakes, the dock, and the Lost & Found shelf.
- **1 / 2 / 3:** select a sorting bin, or click it.
- **Esc:** pause / resume. In fullscreen, your browser may also use Esc to exit fullscreen.

Collect all **72 leftovers**, sort your whole bag at the bottom-left dock, and return all **four keepsakes** to the shelf. Do it before the six-minute sunrise timer ends, without filling the noise meter.

Sorting leftovers correctly earns scrap for a larger bag, a wider nozzle, and a quieter motor. Release the vacuum to give your host a chance to settle back into deep sleep. Story reading, sorting, and upgrades pause the clock.

Click **Clock in** to begin. The game requests fullscreen when supported. Use the player's fullscreen button if the embed does not allow it. Sound can be turned off in the header. No account, download, or API key is needed to play in the browser.

## Jam theme: LEFTOVERS

Leftover party waste fuels your upgrades. Personal belongings left behind tell a small story about friendship, change, and the meaning of home.

## Limitation: ONE ROOM

The entire game happens in one living room. All service stations, collectibles, and the sleeping host share the same scene. There are no other rooms or level transitions.

## Flavor Cards

**Don't Wake It:** keep your vacuum quiet and avoid bumping furniture around a sleeping host. Quiet mode and careful timing are part of the central mechanic.
